Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how Box for Less (“we”, “us”) uses cookies and similar browser storage on this website. We use only what the site needs to work. We do not use tracking, analytics or advertising cookies.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website saves in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser’s local storage, keep small pieces of information on your device so the site can remember them.

2. What we use

NameTypePurposeLasts
bfl_customerCookie (essential)Keeps a customer signed in to their shop and identifies their account.Until you sign out or the session expires

Sign-in cookies are marked HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict, and Secure when the site runs over HTTPS, so they cannot be read by scripts or sent from other websites.

3. What we do not use

  • No advertising or marketing cookies.
  • No analytics or tracking cookies.
  • No third-party cookies from social networks or ad networks.

4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block the sign-in cookies you will not be able to sign in, and clearing browser storage will reset your theme and request cart.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change the cookies we use, we will update this page and the date above.
