Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Box for Less is a wholesale supplier of confectionery, beverages and fast-moving consumer goods. This policy explains what personal and business information we collect through this website, why, and how we protect it.

1. Information we collect

  • Business and contact details of customers: company name, contact person, phone number, email address and delivery address.
  • Account details: your customer ID and a password, which we store only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Trading records: product requests and orders, invoices, payments, balances, returns and the assigned sales representative.
  • Staff details for our own team: name, email, phone number and role.
  • Technical data: sign-in attempts and your IP address, used to protect accounts from misuse.

2. How we use it

  • To create and manage your account and let you sign in.
  • To show products, prices and stock that apply to you, and to process your requests and orders.
  • To issue invoices and statements, record payments and follow up unpaid balances.
  • To arrange deliveries and returns.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent fraud or abuse.

3. Who can see your information

  • You see only your own account, prices, orders, invoices and balances. Another customer can never see them.
  • Our staff see only what their role needs: your assigned sales representative sees their own customers, and managers and administrators see company-wide records.
  • Our business system (Odoo) is the source of our customer, product and invoice records, and this website keeps a read copy of it.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Cookies

We use only the cookies and browser storage the site needs to work. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

5. How long we keep it

We keep trading records and customer information for as long as we have a business relationship with you, and afterwards for as long as we need them for accounting, tax and legal purposes. We do not delete order, invoice or payment history; accounts that are no longer in use are marked inactive.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed, sign-in cookies are protected from scripts, repeated failed sign-ins are limited, and access to records depends on your role. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your password private and tell us at once if you think your account has been misused.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or stop using it for a particular purpose where the law allows. We may keep records that we are legally required to keep.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date above.

9. Contact

For any privacy question or request, contact your assigned sales representative from the Help & Support page.
