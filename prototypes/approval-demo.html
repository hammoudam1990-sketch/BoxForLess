Approval request

What decision is needed?

This walkthrough is intended to make the proposal concrete. Choosing to explore it does not activate pricing or change how staff or customers use the live system.

Decisions requested

1

Authorize a controlled pilot?

Limit to named users, sample customers and a defined review period.

2

Who may approve a discount?

Confirm role, percentage limits, rejection reasons and audit requirements.

3

What can a customer see?

Decide eligibility, any price display, expiry language and return rules.

4

How should Odoo remain authoritative?

Confirm which records are read-only and whether a BFL-only workflow is acceptable.

5

Who owns go-live approval?

Document the approver, date, scope and safeguards before implementation.

Before any live build

Request written approval covering the exact screens, audiences, price source and discount authority.

Provide the authorized Odoo export to validate real product and customer records; use only permitted fields.

Complete backend access controls, privacy review, audit/history, tests and an explicit go-live sign-off.
